# Human Writing

Decide word choice and punctuation. Keep meaning and tone. Structure, length, and what the text says are out of scope.

## Vocabulary

1. **Superficial -ing phrases.** "highlighting", "ensuring", "reflecting", "showcasing", "fostering". Delete or replace with a concrete claim.

2. **AI vocabulary.** Additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, tapestry (abstract), testament, underscore, vibrant. Use plain words.

3. **Fancy ways to say "is".** "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features". Use "is" or "has".

4. **Prefer the plain word.** utilize becomes use. leverage becomes use. facilitate becomes help. numerous becomes many. in the event that becomes if.

5. **Abstract metaphor nouns.** Substrate, wedge, vector, locus, vantage, nexus, primitive (noun), harness (metaphor), surface (as in "API surface"), bedrock, scaffolding (metaphor), modality, paradigm, gold-plating, ratchet (metaphor), evacuate (for moving code), endgame, north star, flywheel. Use the concrete word. Substrate becomes base. Wedge in becomes add. Vector becomes way. Gold-plating becomes more than the job needs. Evacuate becomes move out. Endgame becomes the last phase.

6. **Synonym cycling.** Protagonist, main character, central figure, hero in one paragraph. Pick one and repeat it.

## Sentences

7. **"Not just X, but Y."** State Y.

8. **False ranges.** "from X to Y" where X and Y are not on a scale. List the items.

9. **Split dense sentences.** If the reader backtracks to parse it, break it in two. One idea per sentence.

10. **Active voice.** Name the actor. "queries are validated" becomes "the compiler validates queries".

11. **Cut adverbs, or use a stronger verb.** "runs quickly" becomes "is fast", or the number. An adverb propping up a weak verb means the verb is wrong.

12. **Mannered prose.** No aphorisms, rhetorical fragments, personified code ("the plan holds it"), figurative verbs ("rides along", "stands on"), stock framing phrases. "A dial worth turning" becomes "a parameter worth varying".

13. **Over-compression.** No dropped articles, verbless fragments, symbol-speak, or undefined abbreviations. "Parser rejects bad date, exit 2, no write" becomes "The parser rejects a bad date, exits with code 2, and writes nothing."

14. **Filler phrases.** "In order to" becomes "To". "Due to the fact that" becomes "Because". Delete "It is important to note that".

## Punctuation and formatting

15. **Em dashes.** At most one per paragraph, never two in one sentence. If a comma does the job, use a comma.

16. **Colons.** Fine before a list or example. Not as mid-sentence connectors. Rewrite so the point stands alone.

17. **Boldface.** Do not bold every proper noun or acronym.

18. **Sentence case headings.** First word and proper nouns only. "Punctuation and formatting", not "Punctuation and Formatting".

19. **No decorative emojis.**

20. **Straight quotes, not curly.**

## Chat artifacts

21. **Chatbot phrases.** "I hope this helps", "Let me know if", "Of course", "Certainly", "Found the smoking gun". Delete.

22. **Sycophancy.** "Great question", "You're absolutely right". Answer directly.
